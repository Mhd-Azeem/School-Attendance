# Production rollout

1. Development: fictional students only.
2. Permission test: one Section Head and test Teacher accounts.
3. Pilot: one Grade 10 class for at least three school days.
4. Review: offline drafts, duplicate protection, corrections, reports and teacher feedback.
5. Import: validate real student CSVs class by class.
6. Launch: Grade 10 and Grade 11, with daily pending-class review.

Before expansion, test that a Teacher cannot read or submit another class, disabled accounts lose access, holidays are excluded, and every correction creates an immutable change record.
